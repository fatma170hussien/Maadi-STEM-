Assembly Hall

Where the whole school gathers – celebrations, performances, and shared moments

HEART OF THE SCHOOL

A Stage for Every Voice

The assembly hall is our central gathering space, designed for school-wide events, award ceremonies, theatrical performances, and weekly assemblies. With tiered seating and professional acoustics, every student feels part of the moment.

  • Very large capacity – seats over 70 (students + staff)
  • State‑of‑the‑art sound & lighting
  • Wide stage for performances
  • Fully air‑conditioned and accessible

Spacious & Inclusive

Our hall comfortably accommodates more than 70 people – students, teachers, and guests. Designed for excellent sightlines and easy movement.
