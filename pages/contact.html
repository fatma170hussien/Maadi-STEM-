Directions to Our Campus

Our school is situated in the prestigious Zahraa El-Maadi area, offering a peaceful learning environment with convenient access to transportation routes.

  • Exact Location: Zahraa El-Maadi, Southern Corniche Road
  • Public Transport: Accessible via Maadi Metro Station
  • Major Roads: Near Ring Road and Corniche El-Nil
  • Parking: Designated visitor parking areas
  • Landmarks: Near Maadi Grand Mall and Degla Club
