Fab Lab

Where imagination meets innovation - A digital fabrication laboratory for turning ideas into reality

What is Fab Lab?

A state-of-the-art digital fabrication laboratory equipped with cutting-edge technology

Computer Numerical Control router

Our Fab Lab is a creative playground where students learn to design, prototype, and create using digital fabrication tools. It's a space where theoretical knowledge meets practical application.

Equipped with 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC machines, and electronics workstations, students can transform their ideas into tangible products, developing essential skills for the future.

The lab promotes hands-on learning, problem-solving, and collaborative projects that bridge STEM subjects with real-world applications.

Our Equipment

Professional-grade tools for digital fabrication and prototyping

3D Printers

Multiple FDM and resin 3D printers for creating complex prototypes, models, and functional parts with precision and detail.

Laser Cutter

CO2 laser cutter for precise cutting and engraving on wood, acrylic, leather, and other materials with micron-level accuracy.

CNC Router

Computer-controlled milling machine for creating detailed woodwork, metal parts, and complex 3D shapes from various materials.

Lab Features

Key aspects that make our Fab Lab unique

Collaborative Space

Open layout designed for teamwork, brainstorming, and peer-to-peer learning in a creative environment.

Safety First

Comprehensive safety protocols, protective equipment, and supervised operation of all machinery.

Expert Guidance

Trained instructors and technical staff provide guidance on equipment use and project development.

Innovation Focus

Encourages creative thinking, problem-solving, and entrepreneurial mindset through hands-on projects.

Student Projects & Achievements

Innovative creations developed by our students in the Fab Lab

Robotics Projects

Students design and build functional robots for competitions, including autonomous vehicles and robotic arms with precision control systems.

Medical Devices

Prototyping of assistive devices and medical equipment, such as 3D-printed prosthetics and diagnostic tools for healthcare applications.

Sustainable Solutions

Development of eco-friendly products including solar-powered devices, water purification systems, and sustainable packaging alternatives.

Interactive Installations

Creation of interactive art installations, educational games, and immersive experiences combining electronics, programming, and design.
