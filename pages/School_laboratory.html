School Laboratories

Explore our state-of-the-art laboratories designed for hands-on scientific discovery and innovation

Our Specialized Labs

Equipped with the latest technology and supervised by expert faculty members

Physics Laboratory

A comprehensive lab equipped with advanced apparatus for mechanics, electricity, optics, and modern physics experiments. Students explore fundamental principles through hands-on experimentation.

  • Quantum mechanics demonstration setups
  • Advanced optics and laser systems
  • Digital measurement instruments
  • Virtual physics simulation software
  • Wind tunnel for aerodynamics studies

Chemistry Laboratory

Modern chemical lab with fume hoods, analytical instruments, and safety systems for organic, inorganic, and physical chemistry experiments. Focus on sustainable and green chemistry practices.

  • Advanced spectrophotometers
  • Gas chromatography systems
  • Chemical synthesis workstations
  • Environmental monitoring equipment
  • Chemical waste management system

Biology Laboratory

State-of-the-art facility for genetics, microbiology, ecology, and anatomy studies. Equipped with microscopes, incubators, and molecular biology tools for cutting-edge biological research.

  • PCR machines for DNA analysis
  • Electron and compound microscopes
  • Tissue culture facilities
  • Bioinformatics workstations
  • Aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems

Computer Laboratory

High-performance computing lab with programming stations, robotics kits, and AI development tools. Students learn coding, data science, cybersecurity, and software engineering principles.

  • AI and machine learning workstations
  • 3D printing and modeling software
  • Robotics development kits
  • Network security simulation tools
  • Virtual reality development stations

Mechanics Laboratory

Industrial-grade workshop for mechanical engineering principles, materials testing, and prototype development. Features CNC machines, 3D printers, and material testing equipment.

  • CNC milling and lathe machines
  • Materials tensile strength testers
  • Fluid dynamics demonstration setups
  • Thermodynamics experimentation kits
  • Prototype development workstations

Geology Laboratory

Specialized lab for earth sciences, featuring mineral and rock collections, geological mapping tools, and environmental analysis equipment. Focus on Egypt's unique geological heritage.

  • Polarizing microscopes for thin sections
  • Mineral hardness testing equipment
  • Geological mapping software
  • Soil and water analysis tools
  • Fossil and mineral collections

Laboratory Safety Rules

Ensuring a safe and productive learning environment for all students

Personal Protection

Always wear lab coats, safety goggles, and closed-toe shoes. Tie back long hair and avoid loose clothing or jewelry that could pose safety hazards.

Chemical Safety

Never taste or smell chemicals. Always use fume hoods for volatile substances and follow proper disposal procedures for chemical waste.

Emergency Procedures

Know the locations of emergency exits, fire extinguishers, eyewash stations, and safety showers. Report all accidents immediately to the instructor.

Equipment Handling

Handle all equipment with care. Never use damaged equipment and always follow proper operating procedures as instructed by laboratory staff.

No Food Policy

Absolutely no eating, drinking, or gum chewing in the laboratory. Store food and beverages outside the laboratory area only.

Teamwork Protocol

Always work in pairs or groups. Never work alone in the laboratory. Maintain clear communication with lab partners and instructors at all times.
